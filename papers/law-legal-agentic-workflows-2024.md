# LAW: Legal Agentic Workflows for Custody and Fund Services Contracts

- **Authors:** Watson, Cho, Srishankar, et al. (JPMorgan Chase AI Research)
- **Year:** 2024
- **Venue:** arXiv:2412.11063
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.11063
- **Tags:** legal-ai, contracts, modular-workflow, jpmorgan

## Core contribution

A modular, reusable agentic workflow with legal-domain-specific tools and text agents, orchestrating plans that outperform a baseline on retrieval and analytical contract queries; applicable to both open and closed models without fine-tuning.

## Relevance to the project

Concrete evidence that reusable, modifiable agent components work on real legal contract tasks.
