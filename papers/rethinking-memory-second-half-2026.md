# Rethinking Memory Mechanisms of Foundation Agents in the Second Half: A Survey

- **Authors:** Large multi-author consortium (lead Wei-Chieh Huang et al.)
- **Year:** 2026 (v1 Jan 14, 2026)
- **Venue:** arXiv:2602.06052
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.06052
- **Tags:** survey, curation, self-evolving, long-horizon

## Core contribution

Systematic curation of 218 key papers published between 2023 Q1 and 2025 Q4, with a taxonomy of memory in foundation agents; documents an exponential surge of the sub-field peaking in 2025 Q4. Surveys self-evolving/long-horizon agent memory beyond the 2024 Zhang survey.

## Relevance to the project

Demonstrates the deliverable covers the state of the art through late 2025; updates the project's existing Zhang (2024/2025) survey anchor with 2025-2026 developments.
