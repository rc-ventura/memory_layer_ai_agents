# LLM Agents in Law: Taxonomy, Applications, and Challenges

- **Authors:** (2026)
- **Year:** 2026
- **Venue:** arXiv:2601.06216; ACL 2026 (long paper)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.06216
- **Tags:** legal-ai, taxonomy, hallucination, verifiability

## Core contribution

A taxonomy of legal LLM agents across retrieval, judgment prediction, courtroom/dispute simulation, compliance, advisory, and contracts; identifies hallucination, outdated information, and verifiability as the key deployment barriers, with planning/memory/tool-use as mitigations.

## Relevance to the project

Frames why controlled memory (freshness, verifiability) is essential in law.
