# Large Language Models in Legal Systems: A Survey

- **Authors:** (2025)
- **Year:** 2025
- **Venue:** Humanities and Social Sciences Communications (Nature Portfolio), s41599-025-05924-3
- **Tags:** legal-ai, survey, rag, continual-learning

## Core contribution

Surveys LLMs across legal drafting, research, reasoning, education, and client interaction, and stresses that RAG pipelines and continual learning are needed to keep models current with evolving case law and regulations.

## Relevance to the project

Supports RAG-as-memory plus controlled update for legal freshness; lists hallucination-mitigation (rule-grounded generation, fact verification) as an open problem the project can target.
